Calculation notes

  • Solar power = PV voltage × PV current. Battery power = battery voltage × battery current with configured polarity (+ = into battery).
  • Conversion efficiency (estimate) = battery output ÷ solar input × 100, only when input ≥ 0.3 W and charging is confirmed. Not MPPT tracking efficiency.
  • Energy = trapezoidal integration of power over time; intervals longer than 5 min are excluded and reported as reduced coverage.
  • Timestamps come from ThingSpeak (UTC) and are displayed in your local time zone.
  • Settings changed from defaults: none.
