Refresh & display

ThingSpeak free channels accept one upload every 15 s; refreshing faster than your ESP32 upload cadence adds no new data. Units: V, A, W, Wh, %.

Freshness & forecast

Between “live” and “stale” the status shows Delayed. Choose thresholds from your real upload interval (e.g. 20 s uploads → live ≤ 90 s).

Calculation settings

Verify polarity with a multimeter: during sunlight with the battery charging, field4 should have the sign selected above.

Battery

Exact State of Charge is not computed from voltage alone. With a chemistry selected, the Battery page shows a voltage-based High / Medium / Low estimate with a disclaimer.
